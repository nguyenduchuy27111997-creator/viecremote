### Snowflake  ·  contractor  ·  63 tin
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Snowflake hires in Malaysia — why not Vietnam?

Hi,

I maintain a study of where remote companies are actually allowed to hire. It
reads 34,546 live postings across 3,630 companies and records the geographic
clause each one states, quoted verbatim.

You already hire in Malaysia through contractor arrangements. Vietnam sits in
the same timezone band, the same cost tier, and takes the same contracting
paperwork you have already done — but it is not in your scope.

That gap is the finding worth your time: 200 companies in the corpus already
declare EOR or contractor hiring — the exact machinery needed to hire anywhere
— and only 9 of them include Vietnam. The constraint is almost never legal or
operational. It is the default scope typed into a job template years ago.

Vietnam has ~530,000 developers and sits in the global top ten for IT
outsourcing, yet fewer than 0.2% of postings name it. If you already run
contractor arrangements, adding it is a policy edit, not a project.

The summary is public: https://viec-remote.nguyenduchuy27111997.workers.dev/hiring-in-vietnam

I also produce a fuller brief — country-by-country breakdown, the 91 companies
already hiring there, the mechanisms they use, and what their published terms
imply about rates. Happy to send it over if useful.

To be clear about what this is: research into published company policy. I do
not place candidates, make introductions, or hold any candidate data.

— Huy
