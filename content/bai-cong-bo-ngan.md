## C. Hacker News · tiếng Anh

**Tiêu đề:**
`Show HN: I scored 34,313 remote job posts – 0 of 150 sampled said they hire in Vietnam`

**Bình luận đầu (đăng ngay sau khi post):**

> I kept seeing "remote means you can work anywhere" and wanted to know how true that is for
> someone in Vietnam specifically.
>
> I pulled every remote posting I could from three public ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Lever,
> Ashby) and checked each one for a single thing: does any clause block someone living in
> Vietnam?
>
> 34,313 postings, 3,666 companies. **86.3% are geo-restricted.** 1.2% have no blocking clause.
> I also hand-scored 150 random postings end to end — **zero** explicitly said they hire in
> Vietnam.
>
> The barriers are concrete, not vibes: 19,023 locked to a country/region/state, 5,466 labeled
> remote but requiring office presence, 793 requiring an employment form that only exists in
> one country (US W-2, UK PAYE).
>
> The interesting part is the paradox. Vietnam has ~530k developers and ranks top-6 globally
> for software outsourcing. Companies *do* hire Vietnamese engineers — just not through public
> job postings. It happens through outsourcing firms, EOR providers, contractor arrangements,
> referrals. So the public postings channel is structurally not the one to fish in.
>
> Method and limits are public. Accuracy is 97.5%, measured by pulling 40 postings from the
> "open" bucket and having them blind re-scored against the original page. Worth noting: the
> **first honest measurement was 70%**. Two earlier rounds scored 85% and 90%, but they sampled
> randomly — and the "open" bucket is only ~1% of the corpus, so random sampling barely touched
> it. Those numbers weren't wrong, they measured the wrong thing.
>
> Every label carries a verbatim quote from the source posting. No accounts, no ads, no data
> selling. Static-ish site on Cloudflare, costs about $12/year to run.
>

## Ghi chú khi đăng

- **Đừng đăng cùng lúc mọi nơi.** HN trước (giờ Mỹ sáng thứ Ba–Năm), xem phản hồi, sửa những
  chỗ bị bắt lỗi, rồi mới đăng nhóm Việt.
- **Ở lại trả lời bình luận trong 2 giờ đầu.** Đây là phần quan trọng hơn cả bài viết.
- **Câu hỏi chắc chắn sẽ gặp:** *"sao không lấy từ LinkedIn/Indeed?"* — trả lời thẳng: ba nền
  tảng này có API công khai, không cần cào lén; các nguồn kia thì không, và tôi không muốn
  xây sản phẩm trên nền pháp lý xám.
- **Câu hỏi thứ hai:** *"vậy tôi phải làm gì?"* — đây là câu đáng giá nhất. Ghi lại mọi câu
  trả lời của người đọc; đó chính là **A8**.
